How a spanned wallpaper gets cropped

Multi-Monitor Slideshow places every spanned picture on your monitors as they really sit on your desk, then cuts it to what each monitor covers. Set up your monitors below to see where each part of a picture ends up. The math is the plugin's own.

The rules

First Lay the monitors out by real size

With "Compensate for pixel size" on, a monitor with bigger pixels takes more room than its pixel count suggests, so the picture keeps its real size on every monitor. Monitors stay touching, and each keeps its vertical alignment from Display Configuration.

Center on the monitor join Centered on the bezel

The picture's middle goes on the middle join between the monitors (the middle monitor's center with an odd count), and on the middle of the largest monitor vertically. It's scaled, keeping its shape, until every monitor is covered. The overhang is cut.

Detect pre-split images Slices, or one panorama

If cutting the picture into one slice per monitor gives monitor-shaped slices (within 3%), each slice goes to one monitor. Otherwise the picture is scaled to cover the box around all monitors, centered on it.

Individual (non-spanning) folders are simpler: each monitor fills itself with its own picture, cropped evenly to its own shape.

Your monitors

Left to right, as in Display Configuration. Size is the diagonal in inches; alignment is against the tallest monitor.

Try any size

Only the shape matters, not the pixel count. Enter a size, pick a common one, or open one of your own pictures (it stays in your browser).
